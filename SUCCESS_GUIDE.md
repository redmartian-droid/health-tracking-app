## 💡 Troubleshooting:

### "Gradle download timeout":
- **Solution**: Use Android Studio (it handles downloads better)
- OR wait and retry - downloads resume where they left off

### "Build failed":
- Check that your Tecno phone is still connected
- Verify USB debugging is enabled
- Try: `adb devices` to confirm connection

### "App doesn't launch":
- Make sure you clicked "Allow" on the USB debugging prompt
- Check phone is unlocked
- Look for installation notification on phone
